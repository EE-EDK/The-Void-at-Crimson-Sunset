## CORRECTION — Global continuity reconciliation

Numbers were internally inconsistent. Anchoring on the two most-repeated, plot-bearing facts — the mother entered hole seventeen **Aug 3, 1997, two days after Alex's seventh birthday**, and the **"twenty-seven years"** of life she bought him — sets a present of **~2024** (Alex born ~1990, mid-30s now; mother died at 32; threshold 2065 minus ~40 years ≈ now). Against that anchor:

| Fact | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iris's wait to meet Alex again | "twenty-**two** years" | "twenty-**seven** years" | Outlier vs. six other "twenty-seven" references and Iris's own later line; she met him at age five. |
| Jen's recruitment age | "**nineteen**" (fall-vision) | "**twenty-three**" | Dr. Venn states 23 to his face in §II-B (authoritative); the "19" was the slip. |
| Years since the 1945 atomic tests | "**seventy** years" ×5 | "**eighty** years" ×5 | 1945 → a present where a 1990-born man is in his 30s is ~80 years, not 70. |
| Kelly's text | "**Mom** found something in your old room" | "I found something **of Mom's**…" | Implied a living mother (she died when he was 7). Keeps the hook, fixes the logic. |
| Photo of the mother | face "blurred, like she'd moved" **and** "smile was clear" | "the kind of softness old prints develop at the edges" | Motion blur can't blur the face yet spare the smile; aging the print resolves it. |
